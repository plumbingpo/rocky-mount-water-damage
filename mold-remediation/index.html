Treat mold as a warning as well as a health hazard, because the hidden moisture feeding it puts your property's structural integrity at risk. Breathing it in may lead to allergy symptoms, respiratory problems, headaches and more serious effects, and the risk is highest for the elderly, the immune-compromised and children. Rocky Mount Restoration sends IICRC-certified technicians to homes and commercial buildings across Rocky Mount, where they contain mold, remove it and keep it from returning under strict industry protocols.

Wiping away the mold you can see is not remediation. A professional job has five parts: locate the water source that feeds the growth, build proper containment against cross-contamination, use HEPA-filtered tools to remove damaged materials safely, treat surfaces left behind with an antimicrobial solution, and resolve the moisture problem at its root to stop recurrence. Our comprehensive approach means mold is eliminated at the source, not just hidden for a while.

We begin every mold remediation project with a thorough inspection and assessment, often including air quality testing to determine the type and concentration of mold spores present. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. While the work is underway, the area stays under negative air pressure, HEPA scrubbers clean the air and our crew follows IICRC S520, the mold remediation standard. A final verification step, which can include a clearance air test on request, shows that the space meets the standard for safe occupancy.

Signs It Is Time for Mold Remediation

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Recent water damage or flooding that was not professionally dried
  • Long-running hidden leaks, for example at a dishwasher line, sink drain or roof flashing
  • Patches of mold, whatever the color, on a ceiling, wall, floor or other surface
  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents

What a Professional Brings to Mold Remediation

Removing mold is work homeowners should not attempt on their own. Without containment, scrubbing or tearing out moldy material releases spores by the millions, potentially spreading contamination through the house and making the health exposure worse than it was. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

A professional job goes after the moisture source too, because that is why the mold grew in the first place. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.

Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Dry wet rugs, towels and spills right away instead of letting them sit
  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • Laundry rooms, baths and kitchens all need working exhaust ventilation that is sized for the space
  • Fix water leaks within 24 hours, because mold needs only moisture and 24 to 48 hours to start growing
  • Use ventilation and a dehumidifier to hold humidity under 60%, with 30 to 50% as the ideal
  • Clean and maintain your HVAC system and change filters regularly to prevent mold in ductwork
